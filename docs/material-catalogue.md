## Basic Materials

These are items suministered by free required as a base for ammunition types

- Shell Casing
- Gunpowder
- Raw Lead (Basic material)

## Intermediate Materials

Using the materials prior we can use machinery to process them into the following:

- Raw Lead & Furnace -> Lead Plate
- Lead Plate & Metal Processor -> Rounded Lead Pellets
- Lead Plate & Metal Processor -> Pointy Lead Tip
- Lead Plate & Metal Processor -> Rounded Lead Tip
- Shell Casing + Gunpowder & Ammo Loader -> Loaded Shell

## Ammo
- Loaded Shell + Rounded Lead Pellets & Ammo Assembler -> Shotgun Round
- Loaded Shell + Pointy Lead Tip & Ammo Assembler -> Rifle Round (3)
- Loaded Shell + Rounded Lead Tip & Ammo Assembler -> Rounded Lead Round (15)

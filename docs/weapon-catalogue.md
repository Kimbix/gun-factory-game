## Shotgun

Shoots a bunch of close-range projectiles

> Shotgun Round:
> - Shell Casing
> - Gunpowder
> - Rounded Lead Pellets

Generates a single burst shot per bullet processed

## Rifle

Shoots 3 projectiles in quick succession

> Rifle Rounds Recipe:
> - Shell Casing
> - Gunpowder
> - Pointy Lead tip

Generates a single burst of 3 projectiles per bullet processed


## Uzi

Fires fully automatic, eats through ammo quickly

> Uzi Mag Recipe:
> - Shell Casing
> - Gunpowder
> - Rounded Lead tip

Generates a burst of 15 bullets per bullet processed

## Flamethrower

Short-range continuous flame cone, sets enemies on fire

> Flamethrower Canister Recipe:
> - Empty Canister
> - Emberstone Dust

Each Fuel Canister provides a duration of continuous flame

## Gas Bomb

Creates a persistent poison cloud around the player, damages enemies over time

> Poison Canister Recipe:
> - Empty Canister
> - Toxic Spores

Light damage, area denial, lingering effect

## Grenade Launcher

Lobs an explosive arc, AOE damage on impact

> Grenade Shell Recipe:
> - Explosive Casing
> - Gunpowder
> - Spark Cap

Each shell is one shot — slow fire rate, big boom

## Mine

Deployable, detonates when an enemy steps on it

> Mine Recipe:
> - Explosive Casing
> - Gunpowder
> - Pressure Trigger

Lower damage than a grenade, area denial tool
